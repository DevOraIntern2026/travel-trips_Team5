# TravelOra

**Discover memorable trips. Book with confidence.**

TravelOra is a Flutter mobile app for exploring curated travel experiences and managing the journey from trip discovery to booking follow-up. Travelers can browse destinations, compare trips, check departure availability, and keep their reservations organized in one place.

<img width="800" height="800" alt="Logo (10)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/79518e00-f27b-41fd-9019-6fba7fd322e6" />


## Features

### Explore Trips

Browse curated trips and destinations, search for routes, and filter options to find an experience that fits your plans.

<img width="600" height="800" alt="OK" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/806586ac-e327-4080-8e07-5223d9b7cd6b" />


### Trip Details

Review trip descriptions, locations, durations, itineraries, departure dates, and available seats before deciding to book.

<img width="600" height="800" alt="2" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/396df883-90f5-4556-afa4-7eab1e92ce3f" />


### Booking Flow

Follow a guided booking process: choose an available departure, review traveler and trip details, confirm the reservation, and view the confirmation screen.


### Booking Management

View upcoming and past bookings, check reservation details, and follow booking status updates.

<img width="600" height="800" alt="3" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5b82c927-4a29-4939-b733-3b31686a7dcd" />


### Notifications and Profile

See travel and reservation updates, manage profile information, and adjust notification preferences.


## Technology

- **Flutter** and **Dart** for cross-platform app development
- **BLoC/Cubit** for state management
- **Dio** for network requests
- **GoRouter** for app navigation
- **GetIt** for dependency injection
- **SharedPreferences** for local session and preference storage

## Getting Started

### Requirements

- Flutter SDK
- Dart SDK
